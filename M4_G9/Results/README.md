# CausalLOB — Final Experimental Results

This folder contains the final experimental evidence for CausalLOB (CSE 516, Group G9).

## Contents
- Tables/: Model comparisons, probabilistic KPIs, robustness results, and statistical summaries.
- Figures/: Graph structure, inference performance, calibration, and experimental visualizations.
- Evidence documentation: Provenance and verification of archived results.

## Evaluation
The frozen Sparse-39 Dynamic Bayesian Network is evaluated against AR(5), dense VAR(5), random-graph alternatives, and DeepLOB.

Reported results include reconstruction RMSE, log-loss, Brier score, calibration, missing-data robustness, and synthetic statistical experiments.

FI-2010 retrospective results are distinguished from independently reproduced synthetic experiments.

See the final M4 report and Code/README.md for methodology and reproducibility instructions.
